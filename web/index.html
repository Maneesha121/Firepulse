Method and limits

VIIRS (375 m) detects many more small fires than MODIS (1 km), so raw counts are not comparable. VIIRS months are divided by a calendar-month ratio estimated where both sensors operated (2012 onward), giving a MODIS-equivalent index. The shaded band is the inter-year IQR of that ratio. The ratio is estimated for the whole region and applied to your area, so small areas are less reliable. Anomalies compare a month with the same calendar month in other years (median and MAD). Detections include agricultural burning and other heat sources. Months before 2012 cannot be checked against VIIRS.
